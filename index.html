Merve Karakas

PhD Candidate in Electrical & Computer Engineering, UCLA

I am a PhD candidate at UCLA, advised by Prof. Christina Fragouli. My research focuses on reinforcement learning and online learning under constrained feedback. I am broadly interested in post-training, RLHF, and reliability of reasoning models.

Currently, I am a part-time researcher at Google X (Tapestry). Previously, I interned at Amazon as an Applied Scientist, working on large-scale sparse recommender systems.

Prior to UCLA, I completed a double major in EE and CS at Koc University, where I graduated as valedictorian of the engineering faculty.

GitHub LinkedIn Scholar Email

Research Interests

LLMs & Post-Training

Reinforcement learning from human feedback (RLHF), alignment, reward modeling for text, image, and speech.

Reinforcement Learning

Multi-agent bandits, constrained feedback, communication limitations, regret minimization.

Optimization

Large-scale, convex, and combinatorial optimization; physics-constrained decision-making.

Information Theory

Fundamental limits of learning, lower bounds, erasure channels.
